Import the school workbook

Upload the school’s Excel workbook. The importer reads the Registration sheets (class, registration teacher, students, subjects and subject teachers) and the Assessments sheets (Paper 1–3 marks for Terms 1, 2 and 3). The other sheets in the workbook (averages, Top 10, teacher sheets and so on) are calculated by the system, so they are skipped.

Marks in the file replace what’s in the system; blank cells keep marks already entered here.
